C011 firmware uploader

USB → Metro 328 → I²C → STM32C011F6. Write firmware, read a backup, or verify a file against flash. Files stay on your computer.

1. Prepare your Metro and board

  1. Download the latest bridge sketch and open it in Arduino. Scan support requires this updated sketch; an installed library example may be older. Upload it to your Metro 328 using Arduino’s Uno board selection. Close Serial Monitor.
  2. Connect Metro A4 → SDA, A5 → SCL, and GND → GND through the breakout’s level-shifted QT bus. Power the breakout through VIN as usual. Do not connect a 5 V Metro directly to raw MCU-side SDA/SCL.
  3. Only one C011 may be in ROM at a time (ROM always uses 0x64). Devices sharing an address cannot be distinguished. For a factory-blank chip, power-cycle it before starting; no BOOT0 wire or ST-Link is needed for initial programming.

C011F6 only: 32 KiB flash, 6 KiB RAM, tested Rev Z ROM layout. Application limit: 30 KiB; the last 2 KiB EEPROM page and boot options are preserved. Keep power stable and this tab visible. A failed flash may require ST-Link recovery.

Not connected

2. Find your C011 and enter ROM

Connecting scans the I²C bus. The page checks responding devices for the C011 seesaw hardware ID, and checks 0x64 for the C011 ROM protocol. Unknown devices cannot be programmed.

First connect your Metro. Use the latest bridge sketch with scan support.

Before each boot-option write, reset or power-cycle only the C011, then click I reset the C011 — scan again. If it is found running seesaw, select it and click Boot selected C011 into ROM. Keep the Metro connected during pending verification.

4. Boot options (optional)

First complete step 2 so the selected C011 is in ROM. No firmware file is needed.

Boot options have not been read.

Option writes unlock after the reset-and-scan step above. Keep power stable; an interrupted option write may require ST-Link recovery. Protection and other defined settings are preserved.

Enable BOOT0: nBOOT_SEL=0, nBOOT1=1, nBOOT0=1 (B0 low = application, high = ROM). Normal flash: all three bits = 1 (B0 ignored). RDP must be AA and BOOT_LOCK must be 0; neither is changed.

After a write, keep this tab connected and the Metro powered until readback verification passes. Disconnect ST-Link from PA14/SWCLK before connecting B0 to 3.3 V. Never use 5 V on B0.
