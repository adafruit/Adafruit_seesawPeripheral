C011 firmware uploader

USB → Metro 328 → I²C → STM32C011F6. Drop a firmware file, then program and verify it. Files stay on your computer.

1. Prepare your Metro and board

  1. Open File → Examples → Adafruit seesaw Peripheral → serial_i2c_bridge in Arduino, or get the bridge sketch. Upload it to your Metro 328 using Arduino’s Uno board selection. Close Serial Monitor.
  2. Connect Metro A4 → SDA, A5 → SCL, and GND → GND through the breakout’s level-shifted QT bus. Power the breakout through VIN as usual. Do not connect a 5 V Metro directly to raw MCU-side SDA/SCL.
  3. Connect only one C011. For a factory-blank chip, power-cycle it before starting; no BOOT0 wire or ST-Link is needed for initial programming.

C011F6 only: 32 KiB flash, 6 KiB RAM, tested Rev Z ROM layout. Application limit: 30 KiB; the last 2 KiB EEPROM page and boot options are preserved. Keep power stable and this tab visible. A failed flash may require ST-Link recovery.

BIN starts at 0x08000000. HEX must use absolute STM32 flash addresses. AVR/Metro HEX files, full-flash dumps, and option-byte records are rejected.

For later I²C updates, the new firmware must support bootloader entry (seesaw CONFIG_BOOTLOADER), or BOOT0 must already be configured. This tool does not change boot options.
