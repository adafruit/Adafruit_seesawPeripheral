C011 / C031 firmware uploader

Update or back up your STM32 through an Arduino USB-to-I²C bridge.

1. Connect the bridge

  1. Download the bridge sketch and upload it to your host board. On native-USB ESP32, enable USB CDC On Boot. Close Serial Monitor.
  2. Connect the host’s default I²C bus using STEMMA QT. For direct wiring, use a level shifter.
  3. Connect only one STM32. Power-cycle a factory-blank chip before starting.

Use firmware up to 30 KiB; EEPROM and boot options are preserved. Keep power stable and this tab visible. Failed writes may require ST-Link recovery.

Not connected

2. Enter the bootloader

Select your STM32 and click Enter bootloader. If it is already in ROM, continue to step 3.

3. BOOT0 recovery

Enter the bootloader to check recovery.

Keep power stable. Interrupted writes may need ST-Link recovery. C031 BOOT0 recovery is not yet bench-verified.

Disabling recovery is not recommended. Failed firmware may then require ST-Link recovery.

BOOT0 recovery: B0 low boots your firmware; high enters ROM. Normal flash boot ignores B0.

Keep the bridge powered and connected until verification passes. Disconnect ST-Link SWCLK before wiring B0 to 3.3 V. Never use 5 V.
